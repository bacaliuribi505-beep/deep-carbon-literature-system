## 摘要

Demand for rare earth elements (REEs) will continue to increase in the future due to its application in green energy.Carbonatite REEs deposits are a primary target for exploration as they are more enriched with REEs compared to other igneous rocks.The carbonatites mostly occur in cratonic and non-cratonic extension settings associated with Archean and Proterozoic/Phanerozoic rocks.Carbonatite forms in response to magmatism in shallow crust and associated volcanism and hydrothermal processes.This makes both magmatic and hydrothermal processes responsible for REEs mineralization.However, there is debate on the roles of primary and secondary processes, as researchers have to choose either of the two processes or all.This study examines and compares the Kangankunde carbonatite representing western belt carbonatites and Tundulu and Nkalonje carbonatites representing the eastern belt carbonatites in the Chilwa Alkaline Province (C.A.P) to understand the source of carbonate magma, its evolution paths and implication to REEs mineralization.The tectonic setting of the Malawi basement complex (MBC) of southern Malawi is a subducted crust of island arc setting.Pan-African magmatism was responsible for the extension along the boundary between the Unango and Lilongwe subdomains, which resulted in the thickening and thinning of the Lilongwe and Unango subdomains, hosting the western belt carbonatites and eastern belt carbonatites respectively.The Kangankunde carbonatite complex was emplaced as a ring structure through magmatic differentiation.Four carbonatite lithotypes were identified including: beforsite, magnesio-carbonatite, ferro-carbonatite, and ferruginous calcio-carbonatite.The δ 18 O and δ 13 C stable isotope results show a wide range of -7.7 ‰ to -0.7 ‰, and of +4.4 ‰ to +26.7 ‰, respectively indicating the magmatic origin and involvement of hydrothermal component of the system.Although silicates rocks were observed outside the complex, suggesting the carbonatite magma formed from immiscibility of carbonate-silicate derived from the mantle.Petrographic and geochemical results suggest that the initial magma was Mg-and P-rich.Two trends of magmatic differentiation were identified.The first differentiation trend was from beforsite through magnesio-carbonatite to ferro-carbonatite.The second trend was possibly from beforsite to ferruginous calcio-carbonatite.
